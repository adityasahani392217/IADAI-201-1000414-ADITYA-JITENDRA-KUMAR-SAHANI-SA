## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Understanding (Criterion 1 — 5 Marks)

Urban parking congestion represents a critical logistical, economic, and environmental inefficiency. Academic research consistently demonstrates that **up to 30% of vehicular congestion in urban cores is generated by motorists actively searching for vacant parking spaces**. Traditional approaches to parking management suffer from severe real-world limitations:
1. **Barrier Gate Ticketing Counters:** Only track macroscopic lot ingress/egress without any spatial intelligence regarding which discrete aisles or bays are actually vacant.
2. **In-Ground Magnetic / Ultrasonic Sensors:** Require high capital expenditure, invasive pavement drilling, frequent battery replacement, and fail during repaving or severe weather.
3. **Monolithic Neural Network Classifiers:** Treat parking lots as arbitrary black boxes, failing when camera viewpoints shift, vehicles double-park, or tall vehicle roofs occlude neighboring stalls.

### The ParkVision AI Solution
**ParkVision AI** transforms standard optical closed-circuit television (CCTV) cameras into an autonomous, real-time spatial intelligence network. By engineering a **two-stage decoupled architecture**—combining hardware-accelerated deep learning vehicle localization with deterministic polygon geometry and temporal hysteresis—ParkVision AI delivers micro-level stall occupancy detection with sub-15ms latency.

### Multi-Stakeholder Persona Architecture
ParkVision AI is engineered for three operational personas:
* **The Parking Facility Operator:** Monitors macro-level capacity KPIs, instantaneous fill rates, peak-hour bottlenecks, and automated gate redirection triggers.
* **Security & Field Enforcement Staff:** Receives instant visual alerts for unauthorized parking, boundary-straddling vehicles, and reserved stall violations (EV Charging, Accessible bays).
* **Commercial Operations & Urban Planning Analysts:** Audits historical space occupancy manifests, turnover frequencies, and system telemetry exported in standard CSV and publication-grade PDF formats.

        │

  • CSV Telemetry & PDF Compliance Audit Exports

### Why Bounding Box IoU Fails for Parking Perspective
Standard Intersection-over-Union (IoU) measures overlap relative to the union of both bounding boxes:
$$\text{IoU}(B_v, B_s) = \frac{\text{Area}(B_v \cap B_s)}{\text{Area}(B_v \cup B_s)}$$

In oblique CCTV viewpoints, perspective foreshortening causes tall vehicles (SUVs, vans, trucks) to cast high bounding boxes that intersect adjacent empty stalls. If using naive IoU, adjacent stalls register false positives.

### ParkVision AI Dual-Criterion Formulation
1. **Intersection-over-Area (IoA):** Overlap is computed strictly relative to the calibrated stall polygon:
   $$\text{IoA}(B_v, P_s) = \frac{\text{Area}(B_v \cap P_s)}{\text{Area}(P_s)}$$
2. **Ground Contact Point Validation:** Wheels contact the asphalt at the bottom edge of the detection bounding box:
   $$C_{\text{ground}} = \left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, y_2 \right)$$
   A stall is confirmed occupied if $\text{IoA} \ge \tau_{\text{occ}}$ **or** ($C_{\text{ground}} \in P_s$ and $\text{IoA} \ge \tau_{\text{unc}}$).
3. **Ambiguity Band & Alerts:** If $\text{IoA} \in [\tau_{\text{unc}}, \tau_{\text{occ}})$, the stall is flagged as **Uncertain (Amber)**, generating an operational alert for boundary straddling or improper parking.
